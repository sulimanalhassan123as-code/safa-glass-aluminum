About Safa Glass & Aluminum

A Ghanaian fabrication business delivering accurately estimated, well-crafted aluminum and glass work.

Safa Glass & Aluminum fabricates and installs sliding windows, sliding doors, fixed glass, louvre windows, partitions and shop fronts for homes and businesses across Ghana.


What sets us apart is accuracy: every job starts with a professional, itemized estimate — exact profile quantities, glass sizes and materials — so clients know precisely what they are paying for. No guessing, no surprises, honest pricing and clean workmanship from first measurement to final installation.


Whether it is one window or a whole storey building, we bring the same care: measure well, cut right, fit perfectly.
